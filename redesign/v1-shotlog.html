Toolchain

What each stage actually does

StageToolFunction
TransferSCAIL-2Carries the performance from plate to character. The load-bearing stage.
IdentityCharacter LoRATrained per character from rendered 3D datasets. Holds the face across a full shot.
RotoSAM3Subject matte and tracking. Scopes what the recast is allowed to touch.
DepthDepthAnythingV2Per-frame depth off the plate. Preserves real scene geometry.
PoseDWPoseSkeleton extraction. Falls back to depth on hard turns.
ReferenceFlux KontextBuilds the single anchor image that decides the character and world.
DetailSeedVR2Restores hair, lashes, pore and weave. A plain upscale adds nothing.
FinishFFmpegConform, grade, delivery encode.
