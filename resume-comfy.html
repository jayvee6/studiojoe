What I believe

Point of view

The interesting problem in creative AI isn't quality — it's authorship. A prompt box hides everything. A node graph treats every model, parameter, and step as something the creator can see and own. That's the whole game.

The frontier is making that control reachable — lowering the barrier without taking the human out of the loop — and layering agentic help on top of the graph without collapsing it back into a one-button toy.

Get that balance wrong in either direction and you lose the exact people the tool is for.
